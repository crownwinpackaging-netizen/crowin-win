How to order

A clear path from idea to delivery.

Each one-piece folding gift box order is confirmed around the selected style, product fit, artwork, quantity and destination. We review key details with you before production.

  1. 01

    Send your project brief

    Share the box style, product dimensions and weight, target quantity, destination, requested date, and any artwork or reference photos you have.

    Helpful to start · rough details are fine
  2. 02

    Review the specification & quote

    Confirm the box size, folding structure, handle, material, insert and finish direction. We can then review the project requirements and prepare a quotation.

    MOQ starts at 500 pcs · delivery in 15–25 days
  3. 03

    Confirm order details

    Review the final specification, quantity, price, delivery destination and agreed commercial terms before the order is placed.

    One shared specification · fewer surprises later
  4. 04

    Approve artwork, proof or sample

    Check the artwork and relevant proof or sample for layout, color direction, structure, folding, product fit and finish. Sampling needs depend on the project.

    Production follows the approved project references
  5. 05

    Production & quality inspection

    After the required approvals, the order moves into production. Printing, cutting, finishing, assembly and inspection follow the confirmed requirements.

    Inspection criteria · align with the order
  6. 06

    Packing & shipment

    Confirm the packed format, shipping method, delivery address and required documents. Dispatch timing and logistics are coordinated against the agreed schedule.

    Shipping arrangements · confirmed before dispatch
