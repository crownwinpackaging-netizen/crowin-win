Materials, print & finishing

Choose the surface and structure together.

The paper, supporting board, artwork and finish affect one another. Select them for the product and folding format, then confirm the combination on a physical sample.

01 · PAPER & BOARD

Wrap and supporting board

Choose the outer paper for color, print coverage and surface feel. Select the supporting board grade and thickness for the box size, folding structure and product weight.

Board grade and thickness: confirmed for each project

02 · PAPER & PRINT

Paper appearance and artwork

Choose a paper appearance that suits the brand, then share the artwork, logo placement and color reference. Print coverage and alignment are reviewed on the approved proof or sample.

Paper appearance and print result: review on the approved proof

03 · FIT & INTERIOR

Folding structure and insert

Define the opening, closure, handle and interior layout around the product. Inserts can be developed to support bottles, tubes, jars or multi-item sets.

Fit and packed format: confirm against product dimensions

Finishing techniques

Details that give a printed box its character.

Examples below illustrate finish effects. Availability and suitability depend on the selected paper, artwork and folding panels.

01 · FOIL STAMPING

Metallic highlights

Use foil for a logo, border or selected graphic detail. Confirm foil color and position on the sample.

02 · SPOT UV

Gloss contrast

Apply a localized gloss effect to selected artwork areas, subject to the layout and paper surface.

03 · EMBOSS & DEBOSS

Raised or pressed detail

Add tactile depth to selected design elements. Check line weight and detail size in the approved sample.

Artwork & product brief→Structure & material choice→Print & finishing proof→Physical sample approval

How to order

A clear path from idea to delivery.

Each one-piece folding gift box order is confirmed around the selected style, product fit, artwork, quantity and destination. We review key details with you before production.

  1. 01

    Send your project brief

    Share the box style, product dimensions and weight, target quantity, destination, requested date, and any artwork or reference photos you have.

    Helpful to start · rough details are fine
  2. 02

    Review the specification & quote

    Confirm the box size, folding structure, handle, material, insert and finish direction. We can then review the project requirements and prepare a quotation.

    MOQ and lead time · confirmed for your project
  3. 03

    Confirm order details

    Review the final specification, quantity, price, delivery destination and agreed commercial terms before the order is placed.

    One shared specification · fewer surprises later
  4. 04

    Approve artwork, proof or sample

    Check the artwork and relevant proof or sample for layout, color direction, structure, folding, product fit and finish. Sampling needs depend on the project.

    Production follows the approved project references
  5. 05

    Production & quality inspection

    After the required approvals, the order moves into production. Printing, cutting, finishing, assembly and inspection follow the confirmed requirements.

    Inspection criteria · align with the order
  6. 06

    Packing & shipment

    Confirm the packed format, shipping method, delivery address and required documents. Dispatch timing and logistics are coordinated against the agreed schedule.

    Shipping arrangements · confirmed before dispatch
