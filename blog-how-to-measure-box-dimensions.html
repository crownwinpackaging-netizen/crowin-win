How to Measure Boxes for Custom Packaging

Record length, width and height for the product, the presentation box and the packed shipping carton.

Quick answer

For a rectangular box, measure the longer and shorter sides across the opening and the height from the base to the closing plane. Write the result as L × W × H in one unit. Use inside dimensions to check fit and the outside dimensions of the sealed shipping carton to plan transport.

Measure length, width and height

  1. Length (L). With the opening facing up, measure between the inner walls along the longer edge of the opening.
  2. Width (W). Measure between the inner walls across the shorter edge, at a right angle to length.
  3. Height (H). Measure vertically from the inside base to the closed lid’s lowest usable point. Check where tabs, magnets, inserts or a card reduce the space.

On a tall bottle carton, height can be larger than length. For a round tube, record usable inside diameter and height and confirm how far the lid extends into the tube. For an irregular shape, send a dimensioned drawing and mark the widest points.

Worked example: a perfume bottle with a fitted insert

Suppose a filled bottle measures 42 mm wide × 32 mm deep × 105 mm high, including the cap. With the bottle lying flat and the cap along the box length, its footprint is 105 × 42 mm and its nominal height in that orientation is 32 mm. Mark the shoulder and cap profile, give the filled weight and show where the buyer should grip the bottle.

The supplier can use that envelope to propose a cavity, a finger notch and headroom above the insert. Final box dimensions depend on the bottle profile, the insert material, wall thickness, packing clearance and any card above the tray. Check fit, removal and lid closure using a filled production-representative sample. For a discovery set, measure each capped vial and mark the loading order.

How to measure a box for shipping

Pack the finished gift box into its intended outer carton with the actual cushioning and accessories, then seal it. Measure the carton at the widest point on each axis, including any bulge, and record its packed weight. Use these measurements with the chosen carrier’s current rules for the route and service.

For cartons holding several units, record the units per carton, their orientation and how they are separated. Compare the packed sample against your storage and delivery constraints.

What to include in your packaging quote request

  • Product dimensions, packed orientation, filled weight and units per set.
  • A top and side photo or drawing showing any cap, pump, protrusion or accessory.
  • Box style and any required inside or outside limits. Label which is which.
  • Insert layout, finger access and space for cards or instructions.
  • Quantity per design, destination, target date and any shipping or shelf constraints.

Frequently asked questions

How do you write box dimensions?

For example, write “inside: 200 × 140 × 65 mm (L × W × H).” Keep all three figures in the same unit and label whether they are inside or outside measurements.

How much clearance should I add?

Clearance depends on the product’s variation, the insert and how the product is packed or removed. Send the actual product dimensions and agree the proposed fit on a sample.

Should I measure a box with the insert installed?

Record the empty box dimensions if you have them, then check cavity fit and closed-lid clearance with the insert and actual contents installed.
