The direct advantage

Factory-Direct vs. Trading Companies

What changes when the company quoting your boxes is the company making them.

Trading companies & print brokers

  • ✕Added margin: the price carries a middleman's share on top of the factory quote.
  • ✕Relayed answers: structure and material questions pass through someone who does not run the machines.
  • ✕Limited structures: options depend on what the broker's partner factory happens to make.
  • ✕Separate suppliers: boxes and paper bags often come from different factories, so colors drift.

Crown Win, factory-direct

  • ✓Own workshop: 5 production lines and 18 production machines in Chang'an, Dongguan.
  • ✓Structure development: magnetic, drawer, lid-and-base and foldable rigid boxes built around your product size and weight.
  • ✓FSC® in our own name: licence FSC-C151675 — checkable on search.fsc.org.
  • ✓Box and bag together: matching paper bags produced alongside your gift boxes for one consistent look.
  • ✓Direct contact: talk to the team that develops and produces your packaging.

Buyer questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Checking a supplier

How can I check that Crown Win is a real factory?+
Start with the details in our supplier profile: company name, factory address in Chang'an, Dongguan, and our FSC licence FSC-C151675, which you can look up on search.fsc.org. Our Alibaba.com store is Intertek-verified. You can also watch the workshop video on this page.
Is the FSC certificate your own?+
Yes. FSC Chain of Custody FSC-C151675 (certificate SAI-COC-004166) is held by Dongguan Crown Win Package Co., Ltd. and is valid until 15 September 2029. Products carry an FSC claim only when the order is placed under the certificate scope, so ask for it in your quotation.
What is the minimum order quantity?+
The catalogue lists a standard starting quantity of 500 pieces. Confirm the minimum for your structure, materials and finishing in a written quotation.
How long does production take?+
The standard lead time listed on this site is 15 days. The confirmed schedule depends on quantity, structure and finishing, and is stated in your quotation.

Foldable boxes & orders

How do foldable boxes reduce shipping cost?+
Foldable rigid boxes ship flat and are assembled at your warehouse, so far more boxes fit in each carton than fixed rigid boxes. Ask us for a packed-carton comparison for your size before you estimate the saving.
Can you make inserts for bottles, jars and candles?+
Yes. Share the exact product dimensions and set layout, and the insert is developed with the box so each item sits firmly.
Can I order matching paper bags?+
Yes. Share the packed gift-box dimensions so bag size, handles, printing and finish can be coordinated.
How is the sample approved?+
Check structure, product fit, artwork, physical color and finishing on the sample before approving production.
