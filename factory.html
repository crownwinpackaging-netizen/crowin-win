Production route

How Your Box Moves Through the Floor

Handmade rigid and foldable boxes are built in this order. Each step is signed off against your approved sample.

StepWhat happens on the floor
1 · Dieline & mockupStructure drawn around your product dimensions; artwork shown on a 3D mockup before anything is cut.
2 · PrintingWrapping paper printed with your artwork; colour checked against the approved proof.
3 · Lamination & finishingMatt or gloss lamination, foil stamping, embossing or spot UV on the printed sheet.
4 · Board cuttingGreyboard cut and creased to the dieline; for foldable boxes the fold lines and magnet positions are set here.
5 · Wrapping by handPrinted paper wrapped onto the board — the step that decides whether corners and edges look premium.
6 · Magnets & insertsMagnets fitted, ribbon attached, foam, EVA or paperboard inserts cut and placed.
7 · InspectionStructure, colour, fit and finishing checked against the approved sample.
8 · PackingBoxes counted, flat-packed where the structure allows, and packed into export cartons.

Quality

What Gets Checked, and When

Ask for each of these in writing when you place the order — they are the checkpoints that decide whether the delivery matches the sample.

Before production

Physical sample approved for structure, product fit, colour, artwork and finishing.

During production

First-off box checked against the approved sample before the run continues.

Before packing

Structure, closure, insert fit and print defects checked box by box on the assembly line.

Before shipping

Quantity, carton count, gross weight and carton marking confirmed against your order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you the actual factory, not a trading company?+

Can I see the factory or watch my order being made?+
Yes. Ask for a video walk-through of your specific order on the production floor, or visit us in Chang’an, Dongguan.
Is any part of production subcontracted?+
No. Printing and lamination, die-cutting, board wrapping, hand assembly, insert fitting and export packing all happen at the one address on our FSC certificate.

Yes — not a group member listing or a trading partner’s certificate. Check the licence and scope on search.fsc.org.
