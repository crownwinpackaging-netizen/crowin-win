Plan your packaging

Choose the rigid box structure for your product

A rigid box uses a shaped board structure covered with a printed or specialty wrap. The opening style, board construction, insert and finishing should be selected together for the product and its intended use.

Lid and base

A separate lid lifts away from the base. Discuss lid depth, opening clearance and insert height for your beauty or fragrance set.

Magnetic closure

A hinged lid closes with concealed magnets. Review custom magnetic closure boxes for coordinated gift sets.

Drawer and foldable options

Compare sliding drawer boxes for a pull-out presentation and collapsible rigid boxes when flat packing suits your workflow.

What changes a custom rigid box quotation?

Finished dimensions, order quantity per design, board and wrap, print coverage, insert complexity, finishes and delivery method all affect the quotation. Compare suppliers using the same written specification.

What should you check on the sample?

Check product fit, lid clearance, artwork placement, physical color, finish registration and the packing method. A presentation box may also need a separate protective shipping carton.

Buyer questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize size, color and inserts?+
Yes. Share product dimensions, quantities, artwork and the preferred opening style. Materials, print, finishes and inserts are selected around your project.
What is the minimum order quantity?+
Our catalogue lists a standard starting quantity of 500 pieces. The final minimum depends on structure, materials and specification; ask for a project-specific quotation.
Will a foldable box reduce shipping costs?+
A suitable flat-pack structure reduces empty-box volume. Actual freight depends on packed carton dimensions, weight, quantity, destination and transport method. Request a like-for-like packing comparison.
Can I order matching paper bags?+
Yes. Include paper bags in your brief so dimensions, handles, print and finishes can be coordinated with your gift boxes.
How do we confirm color and quality?+
Confirm the artwork, physical materials, structure, insert fit and finish through an approved sample. Critical brand colors should use an agreed physical reference.
