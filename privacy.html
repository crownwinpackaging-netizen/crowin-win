Privacy Policy

How Crown Win handles the information you share with us. Last updated: September 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Dongguan Crown Win Package Co., Ltd., Floor 2, Building C, Shunchang Industrial Park, No. 6 Shichang Road, Xiaobian 2nd Industrial Zone, Chang'an Town, Dongguan, Guangdong, China. Contact: kevinlu@box-label.com.

What we collect

The quote form on this website does not send data to our servers. It prepares an email or a text you copy, and you decide whether to send it. When you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you include, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, delivery country, product information and any files you attach.

How we use it

We use these details only to answer your enquiry, prepare quotations and samples, produce and ship your order, and keep records required for our business. We do not sell your information and we do not add you to newsletters.

Cookies and analytics

At the date above this website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. If we add website analytics, this page will be updated before it goes live.

Sharing

We share details only where needed to fulfil your order, for example with logistics providers for delivery, or where the law requires it.

Retention and your rights

We keep enquiry and order records for as long as needed for the purposes above. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by writing to kevinlu@box-label.com.
