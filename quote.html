Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a custom packaging project?+
Share your product dimensions, weight, quantity, artwork and target delivery date — we develop the structure and full specification from there.
Can you develop a structure from scratch, without an existing dieline?+
Yes. Send the product itself, or its dimensions, and we develop the structure, insert and dieline around it before anything is cut.
What do you need before quoting a price?+
Product dimensions, quantity, structure or a reference sample, artwork, and delivery destination — confirmed together in a written quotation.
Can I order matching paper bags with my boxes?+
Yes. Include paper bags in your brief so dimensions, handles, print and finishes can be coordinated with your gift boxes.
How do we confirm color and quality before production?+
Through an approved physical sample — artwork, materials, structure, insert fit and finish all checked against it. Critical brand colors should use an agreed physical reference.
